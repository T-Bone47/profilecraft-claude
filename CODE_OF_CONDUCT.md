# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in ProfileCraft should be respectful, constructive and focused on improving the project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, constructive and specific when giving feedback.
- Respect differing experiences and beginner questions.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Protect privacy: do not post private resumes, profile URLs, credentials or personal details without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, or advocating unsafe account access is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and close discussions that violate this code. Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainer through GitHub; do not include sensitive personal information in public reports. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and fairly.
